# **App Name**: QuestGen AI

## Core Features:

- AI Survey Creation: Allow users to generate survey structures by uploading documents, natural language prompts, or through an AI chatbot tool, converting diverse inputs into a structured survey format.
- Interactive Survey Editor: A visual editor to define survey questions, response types (multiple choice, open-ended, Likert scale), and implement conditional skip logic between questions.
- AI Question Enhancement: Provide an AI tool that suggests improvements or alternative phrasing for survey questions to enhance clarity and effectiveness.
- Mobile-First Respondent UI: A responsive survey-taking experience optimized for mobile devices, featuring smooth transitions and clear presentation of questions.
- Data Reporting & Analytics Dashboard: An administrative dashboard presenting key survey metrics, quota usage, and aggregated data visualizations.
- AI Data Bouncer for Quality Control: An AI-powered tool to identify and filter out low-quality survey responses, such as speed traps or straight-lining, from collected data.
- Survey Data Paywall & Checkout: Display a preview of aggregated survey results behind a paywall, offering a checkout process to unlock full data access.

## Style Guidelines:

- Color scheme: Dark theme, with subtle shifts in hue to evoke intelligence and sophistication. The overall feel will be tech-forward and clean.
- Primary accent: A vibrant violet (#BA4DE6) is used for key interactive elements, branding, and to highlight important information, signaling creativity and digital innovation. (HSL: 270, 80%, 60%)
- Background: A very dark, subtly violet-tinted grey (#1B1A1E) provides a modern and focused canvas for content, designed to reduce eye strain in digital environments. (HSL: 270, 15%, 12%)
- Secondary accent: A bright indigo (#6969F0) offers strong contrast and complements the primary violet, used for secondary call-to-actions, distinctions in data visualization, and interactive states. (HSL: 240, 75%, 65%)
- Headline and body font: 'Geist' (sans-serif) for its modern, clear, and highly legible appearance on digital interfaces. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Minimalist, clean line icons (outline or duotone style) are favored for clear communication across all interaction points, including navigation, feature differentiation, and user feedback elements. Icons will be easily discernible against the dark theme.
- Responsive, grid-based layouts are paramount. The application ensures seamless adaptation from desktop views (multi-column layouts like the 3-column editor and admin sidebar) to mobile experiences (single-column stacks, full-screen elements, and bottom navigation).
- Subtle and purposeful animations, including Lottie for onboarding and parsing feedback, smooth hover effects, skeletal loaders, and interactive transitions, will enhance the user experience by providing clear visual feedback without distraction.
